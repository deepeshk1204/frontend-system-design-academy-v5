# Citations

Load-bearing claims live in the topic files, next to the mechanism they support — not in a separate bibliography that drifts. Authors used in the AI and distributed-systems tracks include Liu et al. (lost-in-the-middle, 2023), Cormack et al. (RRF, 2009), Malkov & Yashunin (HNSW), Anthropic (contextual retrieval, 2024), Willison (lethal trifecta), and current vLLM / OpenTelemetry GenAI docs.

Commercial figures (token prices, cache discounts, CDN hit ratios) are **orders of magnitude**. Check current vendor terms before you quote them in an interview as fact.

If you add a number that a hiring manager could call you on, attribute it in the same paragraph.
